# Project Brief: HR Workforce & Performance MIS Reporting System

## Business problem

HR teams need a concise and repeatable way to review workforce size, employment status, department composition, and selected employee performance indicators. Raw employee-level data is not always convenient for routine management review.

## Project objective

Build an Excel-based MIS reporting workbook that converts a fictional HR dataset into a structured summary and an interactive dashboard.

## Stakeholders

- HR operations and HR reporting teams
- Department managers
- Management reviewing workforce indicators

## Scope

The project includes:
- Reviewing and preparing the source dataset
- Checking employee identifiers and relevant missing values
- Summarizing workforce headcount and employment status
- Comparing department headcount, salary, absences, engagement, and satisfaction
- Summarizing performance categories
- Creating an interactive Excel dashboard with a Department slicer
- Documenting assumptions and limitations

## Deliverables

1. Excel workbook with `Raw_Data`, `Clean_Data`, `KPI_Summary`, `Department_MIS`, and `HR_Dashboard` sheets
2. KPI summary and department-level PivotTables
3. Interactive dashboard with KPI cards and charts
4. README, project documentation, and dashboard screenshot

## Success criteria

- Reported headcount reconciles to the 311 employee records in the workbook.
- Active and terminated counts reconcile to 207 and 104 respectively.
- Department headcount totals reconcile to 311.
- KPI definitions and status categories are documented.
- The Department slicer filters the intended dashboard visuals and KPI cards.
- The public portfolio copy excludes unnecessary personal identifiers.

## Tools

Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Excel Tables, formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and conditional formatting.

## Data source

Human Resources Data Set by Rich Huebner: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rhuebner/human-resources-data-set

The dataset is described as fictional. Check the current dataset license and attribution requirements before redistribution.
